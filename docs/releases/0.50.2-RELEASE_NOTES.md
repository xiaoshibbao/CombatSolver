# 0.50.2 本地多人适配版

本地构建，适用于游戏 0.111.0，未上传上游发布渠道。

## 简体中文

- 支持 2～4 人战斗，房主和加入者均可计算并执行自己的当前回合；队友无需安装求解器。
- 加入多人专用卡、队友目标、跨玩家能力、资源、生成牌、球和回合效果的模拟。
- 提供输出、防守、启动方案及独立的多人搜索深度和时间设置。新回合重新计算，预测假定队友不再主动出牌，已有被动效果仍会结算。
- 队友改变战场后重评估剩余动作，目标失效时暂停，并提示共享随机变化可能导致的预测偏差。
- 按实际参与者处理额外回合，本地玩家不会在队友独占的额外回合中提前行动。
- 单人保留原有整场搜索与路线复用。

## English

- Support 2–4 player combat for hosts and clients. Plan and execute your own current turn; teammates do not need CombatSolver.
- Simulate multiplayer cards, ally targets, shared effects, resources, generated cards, orbs and turn effects.
- Offer output, defense and setup plans with separate multiplayer depth and time settings. Replan each turn. Predictions assume no further active teammate plays while retaining passive effects.
- Reevaluate remaining actions after external changes, pause for invalid targets and indicate shared randomness deviations.
- Resolve extra turns for their actual participants and prevent local actions during teammate-only extra turns.
- Preserve full-battle search and route reuse in single-player mode.
